Dynamic viewBox framing · independent pan/zoom · split-pane coordination. No raster crops anywhere in this file.

The geometry on this page was drawn for the demonstration. It is not extracted from 212109 A204 GROUND FLOOR PLAN.pdf or 212109 A801 WASHROOM DETAILS.pdf and must not be read as those sheets or cited as evidence about the project. The room numbers, the 1/A801 callout and automobile elevator 110 are real — they were read off the source title blocks and annotations — but their positions and dimensions here are invented. The real desk serves the real vectors emitted by tools/render_nipigon_assets.py.

Two figures that disagree, both drawn. The solid envelope is 1500 mm, as this demo was briefed. The dashed ring is 1900 mm — what the verified note on the real A204 records for room 104. Neither was quietly dropped: showing the disagreement is what a coordination desk is for. Press the envelope to hold it.

Why this is not a picture. Every framing above is the same SVG with a different viewBox — four numbers. Nothing re-renders, nothing re-fetches, and there is no second image of any region that could disagree with the first. That is the whole reason the real desk stopped cutting fixed-dpi crops: a crop is a way of looking, not a file, and a file can be wrong on its own. Strokes carry vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke, so at 40× a wall is still one hairline instead of a slab — the geometry scales and the pen does not.
