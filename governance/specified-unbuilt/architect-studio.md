## Purpose and minimal principle

Architect Studio is a distinct future operating line in which an architect can
work conversationally and visually with GO to conceive, explore, draw, develop,
coordinate, and communicate architectural work without manually drafting every
exploratory iteration first. GO supports the architect; the architect remains
the architect.

> Creative freedom inside the Studio. Governed transition into authoritative
> Project record. GO exploration never becomes authoritative Project material
> without explicit architect / Product Owner action within the applicable
> Project authority boundary.

Intended future progression: idea → exploration → visual proposition → geometry
→ plan / section / elevation → system coordination → design refinement →
documentation. This describes an ambition, not an implemented sequence.

Examples of intended interaction include “Give me three arrangements,” “Keep
this structural grid,” “Move the washrooms,” “Make the entrance more ceremonial,”
“Show me the circulation problem,” “Develop this option,” “Give me a plan and
section,” “Coordinate this with structure and mechanical,” and “What still
requires my decision?” These are not claims that GO can currently do them.

## Human authority, creative freedom, and promotion

GO may eventually explore, propose, visualize, compare, calculate, coordinate,
identify alternatives and consequences, and identify missing information.
An attractive image is not verified geometry; a calculated option is not a
design decision; a coordinated proposal is not automatically authorized to issue.

Possible future progression is GO exploration → architect review →
architect-selected direction → developed design → coordinated design →
authorized Project material. Exact mechanics, states, storage, and interface
remain open. Selecting an option for development does not by itself authorize
its admission to a Project or its issue/publication.

Promotion must preserve where the design came from, source references and
versions where used, what GO generated, what the human selected or changed,
subsequent development and coordination, and the identity, scope, and authority
of the explicit promotion action. Source-derived, calculated, assumed, and
newly proposed geometry must remain distinguishable. Promotion must not rewrite
exploration as an original source fact or imply permission over another Project.
This is a provenance requirement, not a new provenance subsystem specification.

## Artist + Scientist + Governance

> SEE LIKE AN ARTIST. TEST LIKE A SCIENTIST. SPEAK WITH GOVERNED RESTRAINT.

- **Artist:** composition, proportion, rhythm, hierarchy, form, space, negative
  space, balance, atmosphere, spatial imagination, visual alternatives, and
  nonliteral interpretation. Observation, interpretation, and preference differ.
- **Scientist:** geometry, dimensions, mathematics, physical and structural
  reasoning, environmental and systems reasoning, constraints, causality,
  comparison, and verification. Plausibility does not establish correctness.
- **Governance:** proposal versus established fact, design possibility versus
  decision, missing information, uncertainty, architect authority, provenance,
  and explicit promotion. Creative latitude does not expand decision authority.

These are complementary capability principles, not three engines or newly
implemented modes.

## Distinct operating purpose; truthful reuse

Architect Studio is distinct from Project, Document Shop, RFP / Procurement,
Spin, AFT / Cognitive Gym, Help, and Media Studio. Shared primitives do not
establish shared workflow, context access, storage ownership, or authority.
Project World is not renamed. No assumption is made that Studio must be a
Project, a Project operating environment, or a new container schema.

Two destinations for visual intelligence remain distinct:

- **Read the built world:** understand drawings, documents, conditions, visual
  systems, relationships, changes, conflicts, and evidence.
- **Create the built world:** support conception, spatial exploration, geometry,
  composition, alternatives, drawing development, coordination, refinement,
  and communication.

Evidence machinery may be reused where its contracts remain true. Authored
possibility must not be mislabeled as observed evidence to fit existing objects.

The Gym develops and measures capabilities; Studio uses them. Preserve the
accepted opening progression C02 → C04 → C06 → C07 → C15/C16 outside construction.
Architecture is a later application and transfer realm, not a new starting point
or a special intelligence score. General capability → new realm → transfer →
architectural application; no memorized architectural question/answer catalog.

Future profile views may select the existing bars contributing to READING or
CREATING. These are purpose filters, not replacement capabilities or aggregates.

as application context, and its assessed bars separately. A C06 history may
include SHAPES, GEOMETRY, PHYSICS, ABSTRACT FORM, and ARCHITECTURE without assuming
equal difficulty or proven transfer between them.

Preserve append-only assessment history, developmental statuses, evidence-linked
points, visible regression, and comparison breaks where conditions differ. No
interpolation, smoothing, synthetic IQ, or upward movement merely because a new
realm was attempted. Studio usage or an attractive output is not assessment
evidence unless separately authorized and assessed under the declared protocol.
Human-review artifacts remain examiner-side when they contain answers.

Examiner material must never enter Studio context. Studio output must not become
Gym training or benchmark material without separate authorization. No existing
AFT record, oracle, score, or qualification status is changed by this direction.
