# Specified But Unbuilt — GO-LEARNING-01 (Public Body of Knowledge / Learning Holodeck)

**Status:** Named and scoped by explicit Product Owner directive (CLAUDE-CA1D-PUBLIC-LANDING-01
consolidated addendum, Section E), **not implemented, not started**. This record exists solely
because the Product Owner asked for a preservation/future-architecture pointer while directing
that the historical Holodeck contract/delivery-model material (CCDC forms, DB, PDB, CM,
Alliancing, AFP) be evolved — for the *commercial* front door only — into something else
entirely (the ambient "knowledge field," `static/js/landing.js`'s own C1-C3 tiers). The
Product Owner's own words: *"They are simply no longer appropriate as the commercial
reception's primary navigation... Do not implement this broader Learning Holodeck in
PUBLIC-LANDING-01. This section is a preservation/future-architecture directive only."*

## The request, as given

A future, separate public learning environment — GO-LEARNING-01 — that begins with the
historical procurement/contracts/construction material already designed for the Holodeck
(CCDC, DB, PDB, CM, Alliancing, AFP and related content) and is intended to eventually expand
across disciplines: architecture, engineering, mathematics, physics, chemistry, materials,
biology/medical science (where appropriately governed), art, music, history, craft,
computation, and future fields. Bodies of Knowledge should ultimately be capable of
cross-domain awareness, so a relevant concept from one field can help a person formulate a
better question in another.

## What must be preserved, and where it already lives

The historical Holodeck source material is **not part of this repository** — it lives entirely
outside it, at `C:\Archiosk\holodeck\` (a sibling location on the same local machine, with its
own `archive\` subdirectory holding the full iteration history: `archiosk_holodeck_v_3.html`,
the DB/PDB/CM environment-room scenes, the fire-beetle agent, the DBB haptic console, and every
numbered version between). Nothing in this repository has ever copied, modified, or deleted any
of that material — `PUBLIC-LANDING-01` only *read* two specific files from it (`archiosk_holodeck_v_3.html`
and `archiosk-holodeck.before-RESTORE_BASELINE_V3_ATOMIC_HOME_WORDPRESS_BLOCK_ONLY...html`) as
direct design-reference artifacts, per the Product Owner's own explicit instruction to inspect
them rather than approximate from memory. Preserving it requires no action from this repository
at all — it already exists, untouched, at that external location. This record exists only so a
future session doesn't need to rediscover that fact, or assume the material was lost because
`archiosk.com`'s public front door no longer surfaces the fish-navigation form of it.

## What was actually built instead (PUBLIC-LANDING-01's own commercial evolution)

Not a preservation of the old page, but a stated evolution of its *underlying idea* for the
commercial reception specifically: the moving field now suggests "many forms of human knowledge
occupy one shared environment" rather than a procurement-delivery-model choice screen. Three
sparse, low-opacity tiers (small knowledge-particle glyphs — numbers, real math/musical
notation; real, non-invented writing-system fragments meaning "knowledge" across several
languages; and occasional GO domain-intelligence terms) drift slowly through the front door's
background, behind the commercial hero content, never as navigation. See `static/js/landing.js`'s
own header comment for the exact character sets and sourcing reasoning.

## Why this is deferred, not built

GO-LEARNING-01 as actually described — a full second public surface with governed,
cross-domain educational content spanning many disciplines — is a substantial, separate
product surface, not a landing-page decoration. Building it now would be exactly the kind of
scope expansion this repository's own discipline exists to catch and defer rather than force
into an unrelated tranche (`CLAUDE.md`'s own "record the design and defer implementation
rather than forcing it in," already the pattern this session used for the Peripheral Activity
Dots and Per-Item Attention/Review State work). Revisit only under its own, separately
authorized tranche.
