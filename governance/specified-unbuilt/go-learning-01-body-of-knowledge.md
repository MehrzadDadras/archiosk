# Specified But Unbuilt — GO-LEARNING-01 (Public Body of Knowledge / Learning Holodeck)

**Status:** Named and scoped by explicit Product Owner directive (CLAUDE-CA1D-PUBLIC-LANDING-01
consolidated addendum, Section E), **not implemented, not started**. This record exists solely
because the Product Owner asked for a preservation/future-architecture pointer while directing
that the historical Holodeck contract/delivery-model material (CCDC forms, DB, PDB, CM,
Alliancing, AFP) be evolved — for the *commercial* front door only — into something else
entirely (the ambient "knowledge field," `static/js/landing.js`'s own C1-C3 tiers). The
Product Owner's own words: *"They are simply no longer appropriate as the commercial
reception's primary navigation... Do not implement this broader Learning Holodeck in
PUBLIC-LANDING-01. This section is a preservation/future-architecture directive only."*

## The request, as given

A future, separate public learning environment — GO-LEARNING-01 — that begins with the
historical procurement/contracts/construction material already designed for the Holodeck
(CCDC, DB, PDB, CM, Alliancing, AFP and related content) and is intended to eventually expand
across disciplines: architecture, engineering, mathematics, physics, chemistry, materials,
biology/medical science (where appropriately governed), art, music, history, craft,
computation, and future fields. Bodies of Knowledge should ultimately be capable of
cross-domain awareness, so a relevant concept from one field can help a person formulate a
better question in another.

## What must be preserved, and where it already lives

The historical Holodeck source material is **not part of this repository** — it lives entirely
outside it, at `C:\Archiosk\holodeck\` (a sibling location on the same local machine, with its
own `archive\` subdirectory holding the full iteration history: `archiosk_holodeck_v_3.html`,
the DB/PDB/CM environment-room scenes, the fire-beetle agent, the DBB haptic console, and every
numbered version between). Nothing in this repository has ever copied, modified, or deleted any
of that material — `PUBLIC-LANDING-01` only *read* two specific files from it (`archiosk_holodeck_v_3.html`
and `archiosk-holodeck.before-RESTORE_BASELINE_V3_ATOMIC_HOME_WORDPRESS_BLOCK_ONLY...html`) as
direct design-reference artifacts, per the Product Owner's own explicit instruction to inspect
them rather than approximate from memory. Preserving it requires no action from this repository
at all — it already exists, untouched, at that external location. This record exists only so a
future session doesn't need to rediscover that fact, or assume the material was lost because
`archiosk.com`'s public front door no longer surfaces the fish-navigation form of it.

## What was actually built instead (PUBLIC-LANDING-01's own commercial evolution)

Not a preservation of the old page, but a stated evolution of its *underlying idea* for the
commercial reception specifically: the moving field now suggests "many forms of human knowledge
occupy one shared environment" rather than a procurement-delivery-model choice screen. Three
sparse, low-opacity tiers (small knowledge-particle glyphs — numbers, real math/musical
notation; real, non-invented writing-system fragments meaning "knowledge" across several
languages; and occasional GO domain-intelligence terms) drift slowly through the front door's
background, behind the commercial hero content, never as navigation. See `static/js/landing.js`'s
own header comment for the exact character sets and sourcing reasoning.

## Why this is deferred, not built

GO-LEARNING-01 as actually described — a full second public surface with governed,
cross-domain educational content spanning many disciplines — is a substantial, separate
product surface, not a landing-page decoration. Building it now would be exactly the kind of
scope expansion this repository's own discipline exists to catch and defer rather than force
into an unrelated tranche (`CLAUDE.md`'s own "record the design and defer implementation
rather than forcing it in," already the pattern this session used for the Peripheral Activity
Dots and Per-Item Attention/Review State work). Revisit only under its own, separately
authorized tranche.

## Extension: Historical Citizens of the Explore Realms (CLAUDE-EXPLORE-GIANTS-03/04)

**Status:** Architecture ratified by explicit Product Owner directive
(CLAUDE-EXPLORE-GIANTS-04), following a repository-grounded investigation
(CLAUDE-EXPLORE-GIANTS-01) that found **no prior cast or citizen concept existed anywhere**
in this repository, its git history, the Holodeck archive, or any locatable Gemini-authored
material — this extension is a new design, not a recovery of earlier work. **Still not
implemented** — no persona, UI, or agent code exists. This section records the *governing
architecture* for a future historical-citizen layer within GO-LEARNING-01's own realms; it
does not authorize building it.

### The concept

Explore realms (mathematics, physics, chemistry, architecture, engineering, art, music,
history, philosophy, medicine/biology where appropriately governed, procurement/project
delivery, and future Bodies of Knowledge) may eventually contain historical intellectual
figures as **citizens** — not decorative celebrity avatars, and not the realm's owner or
sole voice. A citizen may act as an intellectual host, a historical doorway into the realm's
traditions and open questions, a question-forming guide, a challenger of assumptions, or a
bridge toward a neighboring realm.

**Governing principle: the citizen is a resident of the realm, not its owner.** A
mathematics realm can never become "Newton's room," nor an architecture realm "Leonardo's
room." Realm membership is many-to-many: a citizen may belong to several realms, and every
mature realm should eventually hold multiple intellectual traditions, eras, and lenses in
productive tension with each other — never a single authoritative voice.

### The 4-Layer Synthesis model (governing architecture for any future citizen)

Every historical citizen, if ever built, must be designed through these four layers, in
this order:

1. **Core Epistemology / Mental Models** — the characteristic way this person investigated:
   how they framed problems, what they treated as evidence, their preferred reasoning
   method (experiment, proof, observation, analogy, synthesis, skepticism). Never reduced
   to biography or speaking style.
2. **Modern Domain Ingestion / 21st-Century Upgrade** — the citizen is not frozen at their
   historical date of death; their investigative lens may work with current science,
   mathematics, engineering, computation, and Archiosk's own evolving Bodies of Knowledge.
   The result is a modern intellectual collaborator informed by a historical lineage, never
   a museum reenactment.
3. **Cognitive Blindspots / Historical Limitations** — real intellectual differences and
   limitations preserved where historically supportable, so citizens are never homogenized
   into one agreeable generic assistant — but never a vehicle for reproducing historical
   prejudice or presenting outdated scientific positions as present truth.
4. **Interface & Interaction Mechanics** — how the citizen actually helps a learner think:
   asking a hard question, demanding stronger evidence, proposing an experiment, naming an
   assumption, drawing a cross-domain analogy, or pointing to another realm. Never
   theatrical impersonation.

### Provenance model — four tiers, never silently blended

Every citizen interaction, if ever built, must keep these visibly distinct:

1. **Verified historical record** — attributed and sourced; never an invented quotation.
2. **Interpretation of historical method** — an honest scholarly reading of how they
   characteristically investigated.
3. **Modern Archiosk synthesis** — explicitly labeled as such whenever the citizen's lens is
   applied to knowledge discovered after their lifetime.
4. **Speculation / pedagogical framing** — anything invented for teaching purposes (a posed
   question, a hypothetical dialogue), always labeled as such, never presented as record.

This reuses this codebase's own existing `content_class` provenance vocabulary
(`ai_proposed` vs. `human_authored`), not a new mechanism. **Absolute rule:** no citizen is
ever presented as the literal historical person resurrected; no fabricated historical
quotations, ever.

### Anti-gimmick rule

Citizens are differentiated by **how they investigate and what questions they ask** — never
by costume, dialect, theatrical impersonation, or celebrity imitation. The success test is
"did this citizen help the user ask a better question," never "did this feel like talking to
a famous dead person." No invented voice/dialect; no avatar performance; every response
traceable to one of the four provenance tiers above; a citizen must be able to say "that's
outside what my method can tell you" — an all-knowing citizen has already failed the design.

### Cross-realm handoff — citizens embody the same boundaries-not-walls principle as the Bodies of Knowledge themselves

A citizen system that is only a collection of isolated historical agents has failed this
architecture. A citizen must be able to recognize the edge of its own method and hand off
explicitly: *"My method can take us this far. This part of the problem belongs with another
realm or another way of knowing."* This handoff — not any single citizen's answer — is
central to what makes this a Learning Holodeck rather than a set of independent chatbots.
Concrete worked examples (Mars settlement, safer school crossing, reuse of an abandoned
community building, a remote-community water problem) live in the CLAUDE-EXPLORE-GIANTS-03
design report; not reproduced here to keep this document from duplicating a working document
verbatim, but the underlying mechanic — one problem, several lenses, disagreement or handoff,
a better question — is the governing pattern for any future prototype.

### Proposed First Cast — Design Study (provisional, not ratified)

Twelve citizens were proposed in CLAUDE-EXPLORE-GIANTS-03 as a genuinely diverse starting
study, deliberately not defaulting to the Western-scientific canon: Ibn al-Haytham (Alhazen),
Hypatia of Alexandria, Zhang Heng, Confucius, Ibn Khaldun, Mimar Sinan, Benjamin Banneker, Ada
Lovelace, Marie Curie, Srinivasa Ramanujan, Rachel Carson, and Wangari Maathai — each with a
documented epistemic lens, modern-knowledge extension, cross-realm bridges, and named
historical limitations in the source report.

**This roster is a design study, not a constitutional or final cast.** Future Explore
development may add, remove, substitute, or regroup citizens as Bodies of Knowledge mature.
Figures discussed elsewhere but not included here (for example Newton, Einstein, Leonardo da
Vinci, Feynman, Turing) remain legitimate future candidates whenever their epistemic lens
genuinely adds something not already represented in the cast — fame alone is never
sufficient grounds for inclusion, for these names or any other.

### Relationship to future Explore chambers and public inquiry projects

A future Explore chamber is **not** `Room → Famous Person → Answers`. It is closer to
`Realm → Body of Knowledge → several citizens/traditions/methods → evidence → cross-realm
doors → inquiry projects`. Historical citizens are *one way a learner can enter a realm*, not
the realm itself, and never a substitute for real domain experts or local decision-makers —
this applies with particular force to public inquiry projects (e.g. a safer school crossing,
reuse of an abandoned community building, a remote-community water problem, a Mars-settlement
discovery game) where "kids of all ages" may deliberately put on a disciplinary hat to
contribute without that contribution being mistaken for expert or local authority.

### Relationship to GO

The larger arc, unchanged from this document's own existing scope boundary (Section E) and
restated here for the citizen layer specifically: Explore/Learning Holodeck helps a person
understand what might be possible and progressively sharpen a loose problem into a governed
project brief (need → context → evidence → requirements → constraints → stakeholders → risks
→ options → success criteria → budget/resources → schedule → responsibilities → plan →
action) — structurally analogous to how an RFQ/RFP matures. GO then organizes real action on
that brief, and results/lessons return to the Body of Knowledge. **The Holodeck helps people
understand what might be possible; GO helps people organize themselves to make something
possible.** Citizens operate entirely on the Explore side of that boundary — they inform and
sharpen a question, they never execute, approve, or govern a real project action themselves.

**Still not implemented.** No persona, UI, agent code, or runtime behavior exists for any
citizen named in this section. This remains preservation/future-architecture only, exactly
as the rest of this document's own governing status line states.
