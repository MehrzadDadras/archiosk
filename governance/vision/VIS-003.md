# VIS-003 — Structural Agency

- **ID:** VIS-003
- **TITLE:** Structural Agency
- **VERSION:** v1.0
- **STATUS:** CURRENT

> **This record carries no implementation authority.** VIS status does not authorize
> implementation. **No UI change, visual redesign, template, component system, or
> visual mapping is authorized by this record.** Any future visual work remains
> subject to applicable `GOV-*` records, `CIC-*` contracts, the accepted Deep Ocean
> baseline, and explicit Product Owner authorization. **This vision does not
> override existing governance.**

## Origin / lineage

**Stated by the Product Owner in conversation on 2026-08-21**, as an explicit
correction of an earlier reading of the Bauhaus/Constructivist direction. That
conversation is the first known statement of the principle in this form; it appears
nowhere else in this repository, and **no earlier provenance is claimed**.

The correction, in the Product Owner's own framing:

> Beauty does not come from making the bone structure visible. Beauty comes from the
> fact that the structural member has agency in generating the visible expression.

**One partial precedent does exist in the repository, and it is worth naming because
it shows the principle is not arriving from nowhere.** `static/css/tokens.css`'s own
header already applies this discipline in exactly one channel — colour:

> "Color appears rarely, and only where it carries real information — the token
> NAMES below are what protect that discipline. They are named for MEANING
> (`--seal-red`, `--machine-blue`), never for raw appearance (no `--red-500`,
> `--blue-3`) — that is what stops a future 'arbitrary styling' pass from quietly
> repainting human authority the same color as machine inference just because they
> happen to look similar."

There, governed conditions — human authority (`--seal-red`), machine inference
(`--machine-blue`), accepted state (`--accepted-green`), pending evidence
(`--attention-amber`), contradiction (`--failure-red`) — already have agency over a
visible property. This record is the generalisation of a discipline the repository
already practises in one dimension. It is **not** a claim that the rest of the
interface currently works this way.

## Vision statement

> ARCHIOSK's governed structure should not merely be exposed, illustrated or
> decorated. It should be allowed to act — to influence and generate what the
> interface becomes.

Or, as the Product Owner put it:

> The structure does not need to be seen. It needs to be allowed to act.

## Product intent

ARCHIOSK holds a great deal of governed state: provenance, authority, evidence
sufficiency, uncertainty, lineage, supersession, relationships, contradiction and
convergence, project context, maturity, task posture. Today most of that state
reaches the interface as *content* — something rendered inside a form that was
decided independently of it.

The intent is that these conditions may participate in generating the form itself:
hierarchy, persistence, placement, interaction boundaries, reversibility, emphasis,
density, motion, adjacency, visual weight.

The distinction this record exists to hold:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Structural Exposure** | The interface depicts or exposes technical structure. |
| **Structural Agency** | The underlying governed state materially influences what the interface becomes. |

Exposure is a picture of the machinery. Agency is the machinery having a say.

## Why it matters

It explains why the earlier Bauhaus/Constructivist work felt insufficient. That
exploration was authorised as *"not historical imitation… discover a new
scientific-artistic application language"*
(`prompt-depository/CLAUDE-BAUHAUS-CONSTRUCTIVIST-UI-01`, RUN, accepted), and its
intent was already generative. What the first visual slice reached was still largely
a **treatment** — a way of looking — rather than governed state having a hand in the
result. Naming the difference gives future visual work something to answer to that
is stronger than "does it look on-brand."

It also protects against the opposite failure. Without this stated, "make the
structure visible" is the obvious reading, and it produces fake technicalism:
skeleton graphics, relationship diagrams everywhere, industrial styling that
communicates nothing a professional needs.

## What it enables

- A design-review question with a real answer: **what governed condition, task
  context, or usability constraint generated this form, and why is this expression
  truthful here?**
- A basis for distinguishing legitimate form from arbitrary decoration without
  appealing to taste.
- Consistency that survives new surfaces, because it comes from shared generative
  laws rather than repeated motifs — see [ANA-002](ANA-002.md).
- A reason to keep the semantic token discipline in `tokens.css` and extend its
  logic to other properties deliberately, rather than by accident.

## What it does not authorize

**Mandatory field. This is the boundary.**

- **No UI implementation, redesign, or visual pass.** This record preserves a
  principle; it green-lights no work.
- **No new design system, component ontology, primitive registry, or token family.**
- **No new TPL identity, no TPL renumbering, no TPL merge.** Template-first
  architecture — `TPL → LAY → governed regions → shared primitives` — is untouched.
  TPL remains governed place and context; Structural Agency is a generation
  principle that informs how those structures express governed state.
  `CIC-PAGE-TEMPLATE` v1.0 and `CIC-PANEL` v1.0 continue to govern unchanged.
- **No fixed one-to-one mappings.** "Authority = larger card" is not a durable rule
  and must not be derived from this record. The durable statement is only that a
  governed condition should have *consequential expression appropriate to its local
  context*.
- **No revision of the accepted Deep Ocean baseline.** `CLAUDE.md`'s Deep Ocean
  paragraph and `static/css/tokens.css`'s `--ocean-*` correction rationale stand
  unchanged and are not reopened by this record.
- **No promotion of visual experiments into governance.** The Bauhaus/Constructivist
  treatment remains pending live Product Owner visual judgement, exactly as
  `CLAUDE.md` records it.
- **It does not make ergonomics illegitimate.** Human usability, readability and
  task efficiency remain sufficient causes for a design decision on their own. Not
  every pixel requires a governance justification.
- **It creates no invariant.** If any part of this becomes a rule, it belongs in a
  `GOV-*` record or a `CIC-*` contract, stated plainly and without the metaphor.

## Evolution / lineage notes

The Bauhaus and Russian Constructivist reference is preserved here in the sense the
Product Owner intended it: **not as a visual costume.** Their relevance is the older
idea that function, structure, force, production, material and organization can
participate in generating form. ARCHIOSK is therefore seeking structural expression,
not stylistic imitation.

`CLAUDE-BAUHAUS-CONSTRUCTIVIST-UI-01` and `-01A` remain exactly as filed — accepted
prior exploration, RUN, with their visual-acceptance gate retained. This record does
not supersede, correct or diminish them. It names the deeper rule that exploration
was reaching for, and which future visual work should answer to.
