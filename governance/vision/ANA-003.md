## Analogy

> A great deal of human cognition runs concurrently out of sight, while conscious
> speech and action pass through one narrow serial channel. The bottleneck is largely
> the communication throat, not the thinking behind it. Silicon does not inherit that
> particular constraint — so GO's single coherent human-facing voice need not imply a
> single internal thread.

## What it helps explain

Why "one conversational product" does not have to mean "one sequential loop," and why
that is a claim about the *interface* rather than about the *architecture*.

It also carries a second observation the Product Owner supplied: an unfinished
concern that had dropped out of working memory was later brought back not by
searching for it, but by re-encountering a similar physical context. The useful part
is not the specific example — it is that **a context can act as a retrieval condition
for an unfinished cognitive episode without an explicit semantic query.** The nearest
repository-compatible term is *state-dependent reinstatement*.

## Where it is useful

Orienting a reader before architecture discussion, and as a review question: *is this
proposal widening cognition, attention, or authority — and is that the one that should
widen?*

**Where it should not be reached for:** any argument that a specific mechanism is
required, or that concurrency is safe because brains do it.

## DOES NOT MEAN

**Mandatory. Never empty.**

- **"'Back kitchen' names a subsystem."** It does not. No component, module, service,
  or layer takes this name. It is a figure of speech in a conversation.
- **"The ear-touch recollection is an implementation model."** It is not. It
  illustrates that context can cue recall; it specifies no storage, index, trigger,
  or schema, and authorizes no automatic reactivation.
- **"Wu Wei is software terminology."** It is not, and must not enter the codebase or
  any governance record as a term.
- **"The adolescent kiln / panopticon images describe runtime architecture."** They
  do not. No observation, surveillance, or monitoring mechanism follows from them.
- **"Unconscious/conscious maps onto an AI subsystem boundary."** It does not. No
  privileged 'unconscious' tier exists or is implied, and nothing may claim reduced
  provenance or authority obligations by being 'internal'.
- **"This is a neuroscience claim."** It is not. The serial-bottleneck framing is an
  architectural probe, not a theorem about brains, and no part of ARCHIOSK should
  depend on its being biologically accurate.
- **"Parallel cognition implies parallel authority."** The opposite, and this is the
  load-bearing denial. Concurrency is a cognitive privilege, not an authority
  privilege. Canonical mutation, approval, publication, external communication and
  project-record change remain narrow and governed regardless of how much observation
  precedes them.
- **"Context reactivating a concern also authorizes acting on it."** It does not.
  `GOV-P-001` already holds: selection is context, never permission. A reopened sheet,
  a changed viewport, or a revised document may make a dormant concern relevant; none
  of them authorizes mutation, reopening a decision, issuing an RFI, or any external
  act.
- **"The attention cycle is a state machine."** The watchful-latency → bounded
  attention → convergence → authority gate → release sequence discussed alongside this
  analogy is a **design probe**. It establishes no states, transitions, thresholds,
  formulas, or gears. `GO-ADAPTIVE-ATTENTION-01` already warns against turning its own
  factors into "a universal scoring formula."
- **"Dormant means running quietly."** Dormancy is the absence of compute, not
  low-priority compute. Nothing here authorizes background workers or watchers —
  `tools/dependency_fit.py` and the Bug Eye record both record that this repository
  has no background-worker infrastructure and does not authorize one.
