## Vision statement

> GO's internal cognition may be broad and concurrent; its attention must be
> selective; its authority must remain narrow. Human-facing coherence should not
> require machine-internal serialization.

## Product intent

Human cognition does a great deal concurrently, but conscious communication and
action pass through a narrow serial channel — one spoken stream, one written stream,
one narrated thread. That constraint belongs to the communication throat, not
necessarily to the cognition behind it.

ARCHIOSK's human-facing Composer is serial because people are. **That is a fact about
the interface, not a requirement on what sits behind it.** GO need not be designed as
one sequential agent loop merely because its output is one voice.

The intended divergence is **governed, not unlimited**. Unconstrained concurrency
would produce coordination explosion, redundant reasoning, feedback loops, attention
storms, compute waste, authority collisions and provenance loss. Removing a
biological bottleneck must not mean removing all bottlenecks.

Three additions to what the existing records already hold:

1. **Attention release is first-class.** Escalation is well understood; stopping is
   not. Attention should be able to release after reconciliation, leaving a concern
   recoverable rather than either forgotten or permanently loud. No current record
   covers this — a repository-wide search for attention release returns nothing.
2. **Reinstatement by condition, not only by query.** `GO-PROJECT-MEMORY-01` covers
   **human-initiated** re-entry — a person returning and being briefed. It does not
   cover present circumstances making an unfinished concern relevant again without
   anyone asking for it. A dormant concern may usefully preserve not only what
   mattered, but what future conditions would make it matter again.
3. **Dormancy is not compute.** A concern that is recoverable need not be running.

## Why it matters

It decides what GO is allowed to become architecturally. Without it stated, the
default reading of "a conversational product" is one sequential loop, and every
future capability gets squeezed through it. With it stated — and bounded — breadth of
observation and narrowness of authority stay separable questions.

## What it enables

- A reason to keep the Composer serial without treating serialization as an
  architectural principle.
- A stated basis for concerns that neither vanish nor nag.
- A vocabulary for reviewing future proposals: does this widen cognition, attention,
  or authority — and is that the one that should widen?

## Evolution / lineage notes

The **Helix symmetry** is the reason this belongs in the same corpus rather than a
separate one: project strands evolve independently and converge where consequence
and maturity demand it; the same shape is being proposed for cognitive strands.
`GO-HELIX-01` governs the project half and is unchanged. Whether Spin is best
understood as one controlled convergence event among several is an open question this
record raises and does **not** answer.

**Repository metacognition** is a further instance of the same shape and is preserved
as an observation, not a programme: the `UI → route → store → tests → documentation`
chain for Case collaboration silently lost its UI at `484a171` (2026-08-01) while the
other strands survived for 355 commits, and `kernel-object-model.md` went on
describing a Share button that no longer existed. An evolving system must remember
its past without mistaking its past for its present.
