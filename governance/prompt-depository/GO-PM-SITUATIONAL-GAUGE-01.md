## Confirmed principles

- The gauge supports situational awareness, not fake project-health scoring.
- It surfaces what currently deserves PM attention.
- It may reflect active Spin focus, consequential findings, unresolved conditions, review state, or other governed project signals.
- It remains compact and subordinate to the evidence and work surface.
- It helps the PM orient quickly without replacing detailed evidence inspection.
- It derives from real project state rather than decorative metrics.

Existing governance provides the controlling product rule: **the gauge summarizes; the drill-down explains.** A summarized signal must remain traceable to the underlying project items and evidence that make it true.

## Spin Room relationship

The Spin Room may eventually behave as a gauge-oriented control and inspection surface. Known programme concepts include:

- discipline-priority squares for architectural, structural, mechanical, electrical, civil, envelope, cost, schedule, and procurement focus;
- custom focus areas such as phasing, accessibility, owner-supplied items, commissioning, and winter conditions;
- what-if toggles such as VE or LEED;
- running another Spin with a different focus while retaining historical Spin sets.

These are preserved programme concepts, not final UI, ontology, or implementation requirements. A focus selector changes investigative attention; it does not alter project evidence or authority.

## First Spin and later Spin behaviour

The first Spin may be broad, comprehensive, and relatively unfocused. A PM may intentionally run later Spins with narrower discipline or focus priorities. Historical Spin sets remain distinguishable and are not overwritten.

Current implementation already preserves project-scoped Spin runs and exposes First Spin, Delta Spin, and chronological history. The future gauge does not replace or retroactively redefine those records. The canonical future framing remains: comprehensive Spin precedes focused conversation; historical Spin finding sets are preserved rather than overwritten.

## Relationship to Spin-Games

[GO-SPIN-GAMES-01](GO-SPIN-GAMES-01.md) remains distinct. The gauge may eventually indicate which governed investigative games or focus areas are active or consequential, but it must expose only compact, inspectable execution/provenance state—not private chain-of-thought or fabricated game activity.

## Relationship to Adaptive Attention

[GO-ADAPTIVE-ATTENTION-01](GO-ADAPTIVE-ATTENTION-01.md) may eventually influence what the gauge surfaces. It must not become a universal numerical attention score, arbitrary priority formula, or substitute for present evidence and human judgment.

## Relationship to Composer and Findings

[GO-COMPOSER-01](GO-COMPOSER-01.md) may allow a PM to enter a consequential finding, ask why, inspect evidence, or continue an investigation from the gauge. The gauge must not duplicate the full Composer, Findings, or evidence surface.

## Truthful-state dependency

The specified per-item attention/review model identifies a possible truthful roll-up: counts or groupings of items with unreviewed changes, derived from real state and linked to drill-down. That model is specified but not implemented. Explicit ReviewThread attention solves only a narrower Case/Finding escalation state and must not be generalized cosmetically.

Do not render sensitive states that the underlying project model cannot support truthfully. A gauge without grounded state is decoration, not situational awareness.

## Visual boundary

“Gauge” does not require speedometers, percentage-completion rings, traffic-light health scores, or arbitrary red/amber/green project ratings. The concept is situational orientation, not gamified scoring.

## Programme boundary

This record does not authorize gauge UI, Spin Room controls, priority squares, custom-focus inputs, what-if behavior, attention scoring, Spin changes, or runtime behaviour.
