## Confirmed principles

Project knowledge should remain connected and navigable according to current project context, evidence relationships, decisions, requirements, findings, history, consequence, and the question being asked.

- Knowledge remains linked to source evidence and provenance.
- Project understanding may evolve over time.
- Old understanding does not silently overwrite new understanding, or vice versa.
- Context influences what knowledge is surfaced.
- Navigation helps users move from a current issue into relevant evidence, relationships, history, and prior decisions.
- Direct evidence, derived understanding, historical interpretation, and current authoritative state remain distinguishable.
- Knowledge remains revisitable rather than flattened into a final summary.

## Living means evolving, not uncontrolled

A living body of knowledge may accumulate new evidence, revise prior interpretations, record corrections, connect previously isolated information, and preserve superseded states for history.

It must not silently rewrite history, erase provenance, promote speculation into fact, or merge conflicting authorities without adjudication. Correction and supersession remain visible, non-destructive, and attributable.

## Contextual navigation

Users should not need to know exactly where a fact is stored. GO may eventually guide movement from:

- a finding to supporting evidence;
- a requirement to related design evidence;
- a decision to what caused it;
- a changed condition to downstream consequences;
- a project question to relevant documents, relationships, and history.

The goal is traversable project knowledge rather than manual folder-by-folder search. Existing repository findings show that stable object identity, `Anchor`, evidence relationships, purpose-bearing return context, and project isolation are relevant primitives or constraints. This record does not prescribe a search engine, graph schema, index, or UI.

## Relationship to Project Memory

[GO-PROJECT-MEMORY-01](GO-PROJECT-MEMORY-01.md) reconstructs the project as an episode for closure, re-entry, and organizational learning. Living Body of Knowledge concerns ongoing navigable understanding while the project is active and afterward. Do not collapse them.

## Relationship to River

[GO-RIVER-01](GO-RIVER-01.md) is one important mechanism for traversing connected evidence and consequence. Do not assume every knowledge relationship is a River edge unless later repository-grounded architecture demonstrates that the relationship semantics, provenance, authority, and uncertainty compose correctly.

## Relationship to Composer and Voice

[GO-COMPOSER-01](GO-COMPOSER-01.md) and [GO-VOICE-01](GO-VOICE-01.md) may eventually allow users to ask contextual questions naturally and be guided toward relevant knowledge without knowing internal storage structures. Answers and navigation remain evidence-grounded, project-isolated, concise by default, and inspectable on demand.

## Relationship to Experience Corpus

The Experience Corpus is related but distinct and remains unauthorized. Historical project knowledge may inform where and how an investigation proceeds, but it does not establish present-project truth or unquestioned precedent.

Preserve: **learn how to investigate without learning what to believe.** Cross-project learning remains subject to confidentiality, re-identification, adversarial-party eligibility, provenance, authority, and explicit promotion controls.
