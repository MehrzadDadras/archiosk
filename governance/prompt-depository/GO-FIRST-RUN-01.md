## Confirmed direction

ARCHIOSK/GO needs a deliberate first-use experience so a capable professional can understand what the product is, where to begin, and how to enter real work without requiring founder explanation.

## Confirmed principles

- Orient; do not infantilize.
- Show enough real product value to establish appropriate trust.
- Avoid forced tutorial tours.
- Prefer progressive disclosure to walls of instructions.
- Reflect the real product architecture and governed operating model.
- Do not fabricate project state, evidence, findings, activity, or capability for onboarding.

## Relationship to Surface Trust

[GO-SURFACE-TRUST-01](GO-SURFACE-TRUST-01.md) preserves polished first-use, empty-state, and unboxing behaviour as part of product trust. First-Run is the bounded orientation programme; Surface Trust is the broader product-quality standard. Neither absorbs the other.

## Relationship to Voice and Ushering

[GO-VOICE-01](GO-VOICE-01.md) may eventually provide calm, contextual orientation or point to the appropriate capability when useful. Ushering must not become a forced tour, recurring feature advertisement, or substitute for a legible product structure.

## Relationship to Neutral Entry

[GO-NEUTRAL-ENTRY-01](GO-NEUTRAL-ENTRY-01.md) governs the real New Project/Open Existing gateway and project operating-environment lock. A future first-run experience must explain and use that architecture rather than creating a parallel gateway or cosmetic role choice.

## Existing operational-onboarding boundary

The current pilot operating plan contains a deliberately minimal human-led onboarding sequence for a bounded pilot. That runbook is not proof that a general first-run product experience exists, and this preservation record does not reinterpret it as UI or runtime behaviour.

## Programme boundary

Current governance explicitly states that First-Run/New-User Preview is useful but not required before commissioning and is not begun automatically. This record does not authorize preview content, tutorial UI, walkthrough state, sample projects, fabricated data, gateway changes, or onboarding implementation.
