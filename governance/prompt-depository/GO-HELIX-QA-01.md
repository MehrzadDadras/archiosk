## Governing refinement

Progressive tightening is a first-class Spin behaviour. For each consequential interface that can fairly be assessed, Spin compares:

> expected relationship resolution at the project-specific maturity and issue purpose evidenced now

with:

> observed relationship resolution in the available evidence.

The comparison is interface-specific. A project, discipline, package, and dependent interface may legitimately carry different maturities at the same time. RFP/0+, 30%, 60%, 90%, tender/IFT, IFC, construction, fabrication, and commissioning labels are contextual clues only. Governing expectations come from project evidence such as the RFP, OPR, contract, issue purpose, BEP/DRM, QA plan, requirements, authority decisions, approved decisions, package maturity, procurement, and commissioning state.

## Required conceptual separation

- **Expectation** describes what relationship resolution should exist now and why.
- **Observation/assessment** describes what the supplied evidence demonstrates.
- **Consequence** governs attention and urgency, never technical truth.
- **Evidence sufficiency** governs what GO may responsibly conclude.

Legitimate deferral is a valid assessment. Missing evidence under a mandatory expectation is distinct from planned incompleteness. A lack of project-specific expectation must remain visible rather than being replaced with a universal stage assumption.

## Spin axes

- **Horizontal Spin:** tests the current cross-section of consequentially interdependent strands for fit at their evidenced maturity.
- **Longitudinal Spin:** tests current evidence against governing origin and prior authoritative states, distinguishing authorized change, supersession, propagation, unresolved change, unexplained drift, and legitimate maturation.
- The axes may compose in one investigation.

## Dimensional QA grammar

Dimensions are relationship evidence, not isolated strings. Where current Camel/MM evidence supports it, preserve source, revision, sheet/page/region, interface, value, units, datum/reference, confidence, and direct/derived status. Candidate relationship classes are:

- exact fit;
- threshold/limit;
- envelope/reservation;
- informational;
- uncertain/unclassified.

Thresholds and tolerances must come from authoritative project evidence. Complex geometry unsupported by the supplied modality must be characterized as evidence-type insufficient, with the richer evidence needed identified honestly.

## Investigative inputs

The following are governed review lenses, usually composed from existing primitives rather than implemented as independent engines:

1. positional agreement;
2. interface definition;
3. cross-disciplinary handshake;
4. semantic agreement;
5. consequential completeness;
6. residual uncertainty;
7. revision propagation;
8. authority alignment.

Candidate assessment semantics may map onto existing canonical vocabularies: converged, dimension conflict, positional conflict, semantic mismatch, handshake deficit, propagation lag, stage maturity mismatch, residual ambiguity, evidence unavailable, and legitimate deferred. Do not duplicate canonical semantics merely to reproduce source terminology.

## Governed output

The concise default report should expose, with deeper trace on demand: interface, strands, Spin axis, evidenced maturity, expectation and its source, observed evidence and provenance, assessment, consequence, uncertainty/evidence sufficiency, follow-on game, and—where professional judgement remains—**Evidence → Concern → Question**.

No automatic engineering correction is authorized.

## Prohibitions

Do not create or imply:

- a Helix health score or overall coordination percentage;
- universal tolerance tables;
- universal percentage-to-LOD mappings;
- a hard-coded trade hierarchy;
- project-wide uniform maturity;
- fabrication/BIM certainty from insufficient 2D evidence;
- automatic technical/design correction;
- universal rules from the examples preserved in the Gemini source.

## Recursive proving cycle — seed → soil → growth → better soil

This is governing development architecture, not visual metaphor:

- Prompt-Spin-Game or investigative question = seed.
- Project evidence and relationships = soil.
- Spin investigation = germination and growth.
- River traversal = roots and branching.
- Findings, gaps, contradictions, maturity mismatch, and capability limits reveal the soil.
- Codex cultivation improves the appropriate layer.
- A later Spin should grow farther through richer relationships.

Project North Star is the future governed proving soil; Experience Corpus/Codex Soil is reusable investigative learning. They are related, not identical. A Spin-discovered condition must be classified before cultivation as: genuine project incompleteness; deliberate proving challenge; GO capability weakness; ambiguous/weak proving soil; or governance/authority uncertainty. Do not sterilize proving value by repairing every challenge.

Preserve the boundary:

> Learn how to investigate without learning what to believe.
