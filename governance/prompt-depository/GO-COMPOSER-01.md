## Confirmed principles

- Composer is not merely a generic chatbot embedded in the application.
- It remains grounded in project evidence and project context.
- It preserves multi-turn continuity and natural project or document referents.
- It distinguishes answering, suggesting, drafting, and taking action.
- Consequential actions require the appropriate human approval or governance gate.
- User corrections update working context rather than being ignored.
- Findings remain traceable to evidence.
- Uncertainty and authority remain visible where relevant.
- Normal interaction stays concise while deeper evidence and provenance remain inspectable on demand.

## Finding and question grammar

`Evidence → Concern → Question` is useful where GO identifies an issue it cannot or should not resolve autonomously. Do not force every finding into RFI form.

## Governed next-step behaviour

When one strong, evidence-grounded continuation is obvious, Composer may surface a concise, editable, adoptable suggestion. Examples include creating a task, investigating related evidence, requesting technical concurrence, preparing an RFI candidate, inspecting a related finding, or comparing another source.

The suggestion remains optional and editable. Do not auto-execute a consequential next step merely because it appears obvious.

## Relationships to other programmes

### River

[GO-RIVER-01](GO-RIVER-01.md) may supply inspectable relationship paths supporting a finding. Composer must not invent relationships or conceal uncertainty.

### Spin, Spin-Games, and Helix

[GO-SPIN-GAMES-01](GO-SPIN-GAMES-01.md) and [GO-HELIX-01](GO-HELIX-01.md) remain distinct. Composer may present and interrogate Spin findings, answer “why?”, expose evidence, describe change and related consequences, continue a bounded investigation, and surface an evidence-grounded next action. Composer is not Spin and must not collapse investigation history into chat text.

## Inspectable investigation principle

GO's investigative cognition runs under the hood by default but remains inspectable on demand by the PM. The default is a concise finding or answer. Optional inspection may expose triggering evidence, sources checked, relationships or patterns considered, weakened or rejected alternatives, direction-changing probes or echoes, and how the final characterization emerged.

Preserve only a compact governed provenance or investigation trace. Do not expose private chain-of-thought.

## Action governance

Preserve distinctions among informational response, proposed action, draft, reversible local action, consequential project action, and external communication. Do not bypass approval, RFI, security, tenancy, or authority controls.

## Real conversational testing

Continuously test the internal Chat Composer with realistic Product Owner conversational behaviour: useful acknowledgement, multi-turn continuity, correction handling, project/document referents, evidence grounding, answer-versus-action distinction, approval gates, natural “why?” follow-ups, and obvious next-step suggestions.
