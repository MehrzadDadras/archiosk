## Confirmed principles

- deliberate restraint;
- coherence;
- precision;
- reduction;
- strong hierarchy;
- carefully controlled spacing and rhythm;
- thoughtful typography;
- consistent iconography;
- clear interaction states;
- polished first-use and empty-state behaviour;
- attention to seemingly small details;
- whole-experience design rather than isolated styling.

“Apple Factor” is shorthand for this quality standard. It does not authorize imitation of Apple branding or visual identity.

The governing idea is:

> **Read the book by its cover.**

The visible application should inspire appropriate trust before the user understands all of its internal machinery. That trust must be earned through coherence and quality, not superficial decoration.

## Professional instrument character

ARCHIOSK should feel like a serious professional project instrument. It should not drift into generic SaaS styling, consumer-app playfulness, gratuitous visual effects, developer-prototype appearance, or decorative complexity disconnected from function. The application may be distinctive, but professional credibility remains essential.

## Surface Trust is not cosmetic

Visual trust includes hierarchy, spacing, typography, iconography, interaction feedback, loading and working states, empty states, first-use and unboxing moments, consistency across surfaces, reduction of visual noise, clear distinction between primary and secondary actions, and predictable behaviour. A technically correct system can still lose user trust through a careless surface.

## Current Deep Ocean boundary

Current canonical visual governance remains authoritative. In particular, the Product Owner-accepted blue-leaning Deep Ocean baseline in [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md) and `static/css/tokens.css` must be preserved. The intentional correction from the older `rgba(176, 255, 244, ...)` green/aqua direction to `rgba(176, 219, 255, ...)` must not be reverted unless the Product Owner explicitly changes it. Surface Trust does not authorize arbitrary recolouring.

## Bauhaus / Constructivist boundary

The bounded Bauhaus/Constructivist scientific-artistic experiment is separate. It is not automatically absorbed into Surface Trust and remains pending actual Product Owner live visual judgment. Neither this record nor the experiment supersedes the accepted Deep Ocean baseline.

## Relationship to product function

Surface Trust reinforces rather than masks the underlying information hierarchy. Important project states, evidence, findings, actions, and uncertainty should become visually legible through disciplined composition. Polish must not conceal weak product behaviour.

## Relationship to Voice and Composer

[GO-VOICE-01](GO-VOICE-01.md) and [GO-COMPOSER-01](GO-COMPOSER-01.md) remain distinct. The same Surface Trust standard applies to interaction language: calm, precise, context-aware, restrained, and useful. Visual and conversational surfaces should feel like parts of one product.

## Relationship to PM Situational Gauge

[GO-PM-SITUATIONAL-GAUGE-01](GO-PM-SITUATIONAL-GAUGE-01.md) remains distinct. Future gauges and attention surfaces should inherit Surface Trust principles without becoming decorative dashboards or arbitrary project-health scoring surfaces.

## Programme boundary

Approval of this governing quality standard is not authorization to implement a broad visual redesign. Wider Surface Trust implementation remains incomplete and must proceed only through separately authorized, bounded work. This preservation operation changes no application visual behaviour.
