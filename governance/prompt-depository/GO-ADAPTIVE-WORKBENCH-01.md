## Distinct programme identity

Repository evidence confirms **Adaptive Workbench / Conversational Operational Terminal** is a distinct named and authorized programme (`CLAUDE-POSTCAMEL-WB1`) with its own governance record, focused tests, and one bounded implemented increment.

It is not another name for:

- **Project World**, which governs the authenticated project environment, World/Mode framing, and Spin-Games context;
- **Composer**, which is the governed conversational and next-step interaction surface;
- **Adaptive Attention**, which concerns changing salience, consequence, and attention survival;
- generic contextual workspace behaviour, which may occur without establishing the Workbench grammar.

These programmes may compose, but none absorbs Adaptive Workbench.

## Evidence-supported principles

The canonical Workbench record preserves these governing principles:

- **One workbench. One primary operational terminal. Many projectable views. No fixed panel count.**
- **Conversation operates; views project.**
- **The cockpit is stable. The visible instruments are not.**

Within that boundary:

- workspace composition may respond to the current task and context;
- relevant evidence, views, and tools may become more prominent;
- unrelated or currently unnecessary machinery should recede rather than compete permanently for attention;
- the underlying workbench remains stable enough that adaptation does not disorient the user;
- adaptation is governed, attributable to real state or user action, and inspectable rather than arbitrary;
- selection and visibility are not project truth and must not silently alter evidence or authority.

## Implemented bounded increment

The authorized WB1 stage found that the per-Requirement Adjudicate form was the only major control block rendered permanently across every Requirement row while sibling actions already used collapsed disclosures. It reused the existing `subdisclosure` mechanism so that capability remains fully present and functional but recedes until requested.

This is a real, tested Aggressive Overflow increment. It does not establish dynamic view composition generally, an Operational Terminal architecture, or a complete adaptive workspace.

## Larger grammar boundary

The canonical record audited and conceptually designed, but did not implement:

- formal Operational Terminal elevation;
- operational tabs or pages;
- true projectable-view splitting and container grammar;
- broader variable or collapsible regions;
- Glass Engine;
- delegation architecture;
- perimeter or rail consolidation;
- project-wide Aggressive Overflow;
- broader Surface Trust work.

Those items require their own authorization and must not be inferred as shipped from this `APPROVED` programme record.

## Relationship to Composer

[GO-COMPOSER-01](GO-COMPOSER-01.md) may operate through the primary conversational surface. Composer governs dialogue, evidence grounding, and actions; Workbench governs the larger spatial and projection grammar. “Conversation operates; views project” connects them without collapsing them.

## Relationship to Adaptive Attention

[GO-ADAPTIVE-ATTENTION-01](GO-ADAPTIVE-ATTENTION-01.md) may eventually help decide what deserves prominence or quiet observation. Adaptive Workbench governs how workspace surfaces can present or recede. Workbench adaptation must not fabricate an attention state the system does not actually hold.

## Relationship to Project World

[CLAUDE-PROJECT-WORLD-NAMING-01](CLAUDE-PROJECT-WORLD-NAMING-01.md) preserves Project World as the authenticated PM-facing World/Spin-Games concept. Adaptive Workbench is the workspace composition grammar used within professional project work; it is not a replacement name or separate World.
