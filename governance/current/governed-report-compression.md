# Governed report compression

The attention surface projects existing CaseWorkspaceStore evidence, Claims,
AnalysisRuns and WorkProducts through work_product_export. There is no report
database, new evidence owner, authority resolver or analysis path. The projection
is not saved. Every occurrence retains its original record, source admissions,
provenance and runtime references.

Grouping uses exact structured semantics: subject, proposition, scope, value,
state and material qualifications. It never uses textual similarity. Occurrence
IDs and their own currentness anchors move to expandable provenance; resolver
states, other anchors, supersessions and source qualifications remain material.
Unbound readings from different sources are conservatively kept separate.
Explicit subject propositions can aggregate independent source occurrences.
Independence counts require confirmed, non-temporary, current pairwise source
relationships. Five filenames alone do not establish five independent sources.
Neither grouping nor corroboration changes authority, certainty or applicability.

The default notebook assigns each group one section: what matters, what changed,
what remains unresolved, supporting evidence or technical details. Contradictory
values remain separate and prominent. Already-recorded conflict states nested
inside a result remain prominent too; the renderer does not adjudicate them.
Historical and current, evaluation and canonical, differently bound, differently
applicable and differently qualified records cannot silently merge. Parsing noise
and technical records are collapsed, preserved and recoverable.

Matching and composition outcomes use existing current-premise inspection.
A changed premise marks the retained result REVIEW_REQUIRED without overwriting
or recomputing it. Professional coverage, resolution, expected-next, root-trace,
normalized comparison and breakpoint fields render their committed results.
Retained presentations continue through the existing download route. Expansion
opens full occurrences and their original source and runtime references.

All seven filters and Reload are GET projections. Their URL preserves selected
attention, filter and selected proposition. No selection is stored across users.
Explicit investigation controls remain available beneath the report. Analysis,
correction, promotion and other mutations still use their existing forms/owners.
Presentation grouping is not a claim of complete project coverage, established
physical performance, current investor mandate or canonical promotion.

Qualification includes tests/test_report_compression.py, matching history and
premise-change regressions, and tools/verify_source_review_ui.py
--report-compression. Browser proof distinguishes real local Flask invocation
from authenticated deployment verification; a local fixture is not live proof.
