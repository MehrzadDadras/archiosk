## 1. Purpose and limits

> WE DO NOT CONTROL WHAT FUTURE USERS WILL ASK GO.

The Gym develops and measures transferable underlying cognition, not a catalog
of anticipated questions. Competence means identifying unfamiliar problems,
locating relevant permitted evidence, representing it correctly, reasoning
appropriately, recognizing missing information, transferring useful principles,
and withholding unsupported conclusions. Repetition is not proof of learning.

> SEE LIKE AN ARTIST. TEST LIKE A SCIENTIST. SPEAK WITH GOVERNED RESTRAINT.

The principle supports READ THE BUILT WORLD and CREATE THE BUILT WORLD. These are
destinations, not the limits of cognition. Architect Studio uses capabilities;
the Gym develops/measures them. No operating lines, contexts, or authorities
are merged. Construction and architecture are later transfer/stress realms.

The 28 bars are operational, not biologically independent faculties. No human
IQ, developmental-age equivalence, family average, or overall intelligence score
is permitted. No current GO competence is inferred from this registration or
backfilled from historical AFT results.

## 2. Four independent assessment dimensions

| Dimension | Record separately |
|---|---|
| Cognitive capability | Stable bar ID, target operation, and secondary cognitive demands. |
| Domain knowledge | Necessary facts/terms/laws; whether supplied, separately checked as prerequisites, or acquired under an authorized protocol. |
| Task difficulty | Explicit challenge factors and the capability-specific calibrated level, where established. |
| Execution validity | Whether permitted input, modality, rendering, transport, budget, output contract, and isolation functioned as required. |

Knowing a scientific term does not establish reasoning; missing it does not
automatically establish a reasoning weakness. Preserve end-to-end outcomes while
separating perception, representation, reasoning, and response-delivery issues.
Do not silently discard invalid executions to inflate apparent reliability.

## 4. Developmental language

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| UNTESTED | No valid assessment evidence exists for the stated scope. |
| LEARNING | Initial evidence exists; consistency is not established. |
| NEEDS MORE TRAINING | Observed difficulties identify a specific development frontier. |
| RELIABLE AT THIS LEVEL | Evidence meets declared reliability and restraint criteria at the stated level and scope. |
| READY TO RAISE THE BAR | Evidence supports attempting greater difficulty; it does not demonstrate that greater competence. |
| TRANSFER IN PROGRESS | Assessment in new realms is underway; transfer is not established. |
| TRANSFERRED | Evidence supports transfer to explicitly named realms and conditions. |

These are not one linear ladder. A bar may be RELIABLE AT THIS LEVEL in one realm
and TRANSFER IN PROGRESS in another. Weak results identify developmental needs,
not terminal failure states. Developmental wording must never hide errors,
unsupported certainty, or declining reliable performance. "Needs more training"
does not authorize training or imply model-weight changes.

Instrument, protocol, and release gates remain separate. Historical AFT PASS,
FAIL, QUALIFIED, and NOT QUALIFIED records, scores, seals, and raw outputs remain
unchanged. Cognitive developmental status does not substitute for those gates.

## 5. Adaptive development loop — direction, not execution authority

ASSESS → FIND DEVELOPMENT FRONTIER → LOWER THE BAR → ISOLATE THE WEAK COGNITIVE
PRIMITIVE → EXERCISE IT ON FRESH MATERIAL → RAISE THE BAR → BLIND RETEST → CHANGE
REALM → TEST TRANSFER → UPDATE PROFILE.

Lowering challenge difficulty for development does not rewrite the previously
demonstrated level. Isolate perception, knowledge, semantics, or delivery before
attributing weakness. Use unfamiliar developmental material; never teach a sealed
answer or case-specific answer pattern. Holdout feedback and examiner commentary
must not enter a blind runner. Improvement requires fresh evidence, not repeated
exposure or self-reported learning. Changes and new executions require their own
authorization and any required instrument requalification.

### 5.1 Playful development and orientation

Product Owner direction registered 2026-09-09 under `GO COGNITIVE GYM - C01
ORIENTATION PLAY GRAMMAR DESIGN 01`; this additive principle does not authorize
exercises or change frozen assessment instructions.

> DEVELOP WITH PLAY; MEASURE WITH DISCIPLINE.
> ORIENT BEFORE REASONING. TARGET FIRST. SIGNAL SECOND. NOISE LAST.

Development begins by orienting to the situation, task objective, relevant
evidence, and uncertainty. C01 attention/inhibition is a developmental front
door, not a replacement for the accepted opening assessment sequence or a
hidden multiplier of other capability results. Here target means task objective;
it does not redefine C09's TARGET proposition axis. Observe appropriate action,
selection, reorientation, or necessary clarification, not checklist recitation.

Development may adapt, demonstrate, and briefly reinforce clean success using
fresh material. Record assistance and exposure. Exemplars show richer
possibilities but are not scored, assessment evidence, or future holdouts.
Developmental experience is not itself evidence of established competence or
durable learning. Blind assessment remains isolated and unchanged: no coaching,
cookies, exemplar answers, or feedback reinjection. Answer-bearing developmental
material and its derivatives cannot be represented as pristine holdouts.

Target changes must be communicated; obsolete objectives must not silently
govern later actions. Preserve unaffected evidence and constraints. Distinguish
a changed world from newly revealed information, and target ambiguity from
missing evidence or a difficult but clear task. Reinforce selective attention,
appropriate clarification, restraint, and recovery, not verbosity, confidence,
blanket hesitation, or agreement. Playful surprise must not secretly change
rules and attribute the resulting confusion to cognition.

Accepted bounded specification: [C01 developmental observability](C01-DEVELOPMENT-OBSERVABILITY.md).
This defines encounter records and examiner attribution, not instrumentation or
assessment execution authority.

## 6. Realms and transfer

Realm is independent of capability and of knowledge requirements. Initial realm
vocabulary (extensible, not a closed ontology): SHAPES, ABSTRACT PATTERNS, NUMBERS,
MATHEMATICS, GEOMETRY, SYNTHETIC CAUSAL SYSTEMS, PHYSICS, CHEMISTRY, BIOLOGY,
ECOLOGY, ASTRONOMY, ART, MUSIC, NAVIGATION, STRATEGY, SOCIAL / MULTI-ACTOR SYSTEMS,
TECHNICAL DIAGRAMS, ENGINEERING, ABSTRACT FORM, ARCHITECTURE, CONSTRUCTION.

Declare the transferable relationship; hold out problem families, not merely
renamed instances. Vary surface, structure, modality, and realm deliberately;
control knowledge prerequisites and exposure. Include boundaries where analogy
does not hold. Record assistance, feedback, additional examples, and transfer
cost. Public examples and developmental material are never pristine holdouts.
New realm exposure alone does not establish transfer.

## 7. Bar measurement and cell evidence

Each assessment retains difficulty, reliability, transfer, restraint, evidence
count/diversity, confidence in the assessment, realm, configuration, and
supporting execution IDs. Confidence is not the model's self-report.

Provisional difficulty anchors, requiring capability-specific calibration:
D0 isolated; D1 composed; D2 competing cues/constraints; D3 incomplete or changing
conditions; D4 unfamiliar structure. Frontier challenges remain uncalibrated
until justified. D-levels are ordinal, not equal increments across capabilities.
Retain the actual factors (entities, dependency depth, noise, ambiguity, horizon,
branching, assistance), rather than equating difficulty with prompt length.

Before assessment, declare level-demonstration criteria: reliability threshold,
evidence diversity, uncertainty method, restraint criteria, and applicable
transfer conditions. Do not invent universal precision from a small sample.
Separate repeated executions from independent problem families. Finite perfect
observations do not prove perfect future reliability.

Restraint includes unsupported-claim severity/rate, appropriate abstention,
answerable coverage, and calibration where measurable. Blanket abstention is not
competence; many easy correct answers cannot conceal material unsupported claims.
Open artistic interpretations need supported judgments and recorded disagreement,
not an invented single aesthetic answer.

Compact notation template (no performance values assigned):

`D{level} · R{successes}/{valid attempts} · T{status} · G{status} · E{attempts}a/{families}f · C{confidence}`

- D: highest level meeting declared criteria in the displayed evidence scope.
- R: successful outcomes / valid attempts at that level; partial outcomes remain
  in the expanded assessment. An internal criterion outcome is not a PASS/FAIL
  cognitive-development label.
- T: UNTESTED, WITHIN-REALM, CROSS-REALM, or MIXED; expand to named conditions.
- G: UNASSESSED, CRITERIA MET, or BREACH; preserve severity and explanation.
- E: supporting valid attempts and independent problem families.
- C: INSUFFICIENT, LIMITED, or SUPPORTED, using declared evidence rules and
  uncertainty intervals where justified; no fabricated percentage confidence.

Untouched cells show UNTESTED in full, never zero. NOT EXECUTED and INVALID
EXECUTION are distinct operational states. ASSESSED / D— means valid evidence
exists but no level is established. Keep developmental status alongside these
evidence fields; transfer and developmental statuses are not interchangeable.

## 8. Longitudinal profile matrix

Columns = C01–C28, grouped into the six families. Rows = Campaign × Realm ×
Assessment Date × Frozen Model/Runtime Configuration. Multiple realms or changed
configurations get separate rows. Record protocol version, exposure status,
execution and review links. Unassessed bars in a row remain UNTESTED.

History is append-only in principle. A later assessment or correction links to
the earlier record and does not silently overwrite it. The current-profile view
must identify its evidence selection and may not merge incompatible conditions.
This registration contains no result rows or fabricated performance.

## 9. Cognitive Progress History — Product Owner line-chart specification

For each capability, X = chronological assessments/campaigns; Y = highest
defensibly demonstrated difficulty under comparable conditions. Retain same-date
execution order. This is a VIEW of evidence, never the authoritative evidence.

| Visual event | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| UP | Comparable evidence establishes a higher demonstrated level. |
| FLAT | A new comparable assessment supports the same level; no assessment means no extension. |
| DOWN | Comparable evidence establishes a lower reliable level; prior achievements remain in history. |
| BREAK / GAP / NOT COMPARABLE | Missing or insufficient evidence, or material condition changes, prevent a responsible direct comparison. |
| UNTESTED | No plotted performance point, not zero difficulty. |

No interpolation, smoothing, invented points, synthetic IQ, or cumulative
best-ever line hiding regression. Connecting observations does not estimate
unmeasured performance between them. READY TO RAISE THE BAR is a milestone, not
an upward step. TRANSFERRED is not automatically a higher difficulty. A harder
challenge above established competence creates a development-frontier marker,
not an automatic decline. If comparable reassessment establishes no level, show
LEVEL NOT ESTABLISHED outside the numerical scale rather than inventing zero.

Comparison series document catalog/difficulty versions, realm and knowledge,
modality, tools, assistance, evidence quality, protocol, exposure, and frozen
model/runtime configuration. Configuration changes require explicit comparability
determination before connecting segments. Otherwise break the line and explain.

Every point exposes developmental status, difficulty, reliability, realm,
transfer, restraint, confidence, configuration, supporting campaign/executions,
Product Owner review, and comparison rationale. Regression and restraint breaches
remain explicit even if another metric improves. Use text/symbols as well as color.

## 10. Human-readable dashboard specification

Required views: current capability profile; one capability over time; selected
capabilities over aligned time; realm-specific history; transfer history;
development frontiers; strengths/weaknesses; regressions; coverage/untested areas;
campaign history and latest realm actually assessed for each bar.

Use aligned small charts for selected bars, preserving each bar's own scale;
no radar total or cross-bar numerical ranking. Transfer history marks the first
evidenced milestones RELIABLE AT THIS LEVEL, READY TO RAISE THE BAR, TRANSFER IN
PROGRESS, and TRANSFERRED with named realms. Improvement/regression requires
defensible comparison; otherwise display NOT COMPARABLE or insufficient evidence.

Selection should open TASK / STIMULUS → PERMITTED EVIDENCE → RAW GO ANSWER →
ASSESSMENT → WHY → PRODUCT OWNER VISUAL REVIEW. Never replace the raw answer with
a paraphrase. Each real Take receives a review package; an active qualification
receives one when visual inspection materially helps. Older sealed synthetic
qualifications remain unchanged unless specifically requested.

Assessment and profile views link to evidence; they never rewrite it. Examiner
answers and reviews must not enter Studio or runner context. Studio output is
not Gym training or benchmark material without separate authorization.
