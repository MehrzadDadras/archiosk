{# CLAUDE-PROJECT-SURFACE-CONSOLIDATION-01 addendum (Storage Grammar & Public-Trial Entitlement): supersedes CLAUDE-HYBRID-ENTRY-01's own "Legacy" framing. The enduring choice is Link vs. Upload (Part 3): LINK leaves documents in their existing approved storage and connects Archiosk to them; UPLOAD makes a copy in the Project's configured managed storage. Link is shown, not hidden, but honestly disabled - no genuine linked-storage mechanism exists yet, and Part 4 forbids disguising today's copy-upload as a link.
