Archiosk is designed to work alongside your project's existing material, without becoming a duplicate permanent copy of it — a linked local/company-storage option for that is being prepared. Until it ships, both options below upload and permanently store a copy of your files on Archiosk's own server.

Accepted formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, MD — up to {{ max_upload_mb }}MB each. Best suited to text-based material such as RFPs, RFQs, specs, contracts, reports, and meeting minutes with exhibits. Scanned drawings, native spreadsheets, and images aren’t supported yet.
