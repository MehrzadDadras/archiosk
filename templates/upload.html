Archiosk works alongside your project's existing material — it does not take the place of it, and your files stay wherever they already are. Choose the folder holding your RFP/pursuit material and Archiosk brings in every eligible document inside it; a single file works too if that's all you have.

Accepted formats: PDF, DOCX, TXT, CSV, MD — up to {{ max_upload_mb }}MB each. Best suited to text-based material such as RFPs, RFQs, specs, contracts, reports, and meeting minutes with exhibits. Scanned drawings, native spreadsheets, and images aren’t supported yet.
