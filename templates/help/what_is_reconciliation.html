What is Reconciliation?

You point at the authoritative folder. Archiosk tells you how what it holds differs from what you have. Then you decide.

Compare, report, approve

Nothing is added, relinked, or removed until you approve it. Reconcile is a comparison that produces a report. The report is the product; the change is a separate, deliberate second step you take after reading it. If you close the page instead, nothing has happened.

This is the whole reason the feature is shaped this way. An import that silently absorbed a folder would leave you unable to say what changed or when — and on a project where a drawing revision carries liability, "what changed" is the question.

How the comparison is made

Each file in the folder you choose is reduced to a descriptor: its path within the folder, its filename, its size, and a SHA-256 digest of its contents. Those descriptors are compared against the Sources already registered on the project.

Content, not filename, is what identifies a file. That is what lets a renamed-but-identical drawing be recognised as the same document rather than reported as a new one.

The seven verdicts

Every file lands in exactly one:

  • Unchanged — already registered, same content.
  • New — not registered before.
  • Modified — same identity, different content. A revision.
  • Missing — registered here, but not present in the folder you pointed at.
  • Renamed / moved — same content, different name or path.
  • Ineligible — a format Archiosk does not accept. Not an error, just skipped, and told to you rather than hidden.
  • Ambiguous — the evidence supports more than one reading, so Archiosk declines to guess. These are surfaced for a person precisely because an automatic answer would be a fabricated one.

What Reconcile is not

It is not clash detection. Archiosk does not compare geometry between disciplines and does not report interferences. Reconcile compares a folder of files against a register of documents; it is about which documents you hold and which revision they are at, not about whether a duct meets a beam.

It is also not an importer. It never reaches out to a folder on its own, on a schedule or otherwise — you choose the folder, each time, and you approve the result.
