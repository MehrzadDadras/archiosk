{# CLAUDE-FISH-TANK-01 — one spatial container for every set of things. THE NAME IS THE SPECIFICATION. A fish tank is a bounded volume you look INTO: it has edges you can see, the things inside sit at a depth, and nothing floats out of it. That is the whole grammar, and it is why this is one component rather than three similar-looking grids. THREE PARTS, AND WHAT EACH ONE IS FOR the HUD where you are, as a trail. Persistent, so a person two levels down never has to reconstruct how they got there. the CANVAS the bounded volume. Its edge is visible, so a set of things reads as a set rather than as items loose on a page. the NODES what is inside. Every node is ONE TAP to its destination. WHY THE NODE IS A LINK AND NOT A CARD WITH A BUTTON IN IT A card that is decorative except for a "View" button inside it teaches people to aim, and aiming is what fails on a phone at arm's length on a site. The whole node is the target, it is a real , and it therefore works with a keyboard and with a screen reader for free. Any secondary action lives OUTSIDE the node, never nested inside the link - a link containing a button is invalid HTML and behaves differently in every browser. WHAT THIS DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO It renders no drawing and it must never be wrapped around a drawing viewport. A coordination desk is full-bleed on purpose: every pixel of chrome is a pixel of drawing somebody cannot see. The tank is for CHOOSING a thing; once chosen, the thing gets the whole screen.
